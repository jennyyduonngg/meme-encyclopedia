Nyan Cat


This started off as a drawing in 2011 by artist, Chris Torres who was inpsired by his own cat, Marty.

Two viewers had separately suggested drawing a cat and PopTart, therefore he combined the two.

It was then paird with an animaotion with a Japanese electronic song called "Nyanyanyanyanyanyanya". This video quickly rose to popularity across social media.
