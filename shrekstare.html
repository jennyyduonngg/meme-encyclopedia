Shrek's Stare

Originating in the movie, Shrek the Third in 2007.

This scene near the climax, where the Prince Charming appears to stab Shrek during a performance.

Shrek leans forward with the sword and gives Arthur and Fiona a cheeky smile to show he's okay. Check out the video below!

It became popular when people edited to Shrek's smile into a so-called lightskin star or rizz moment.
